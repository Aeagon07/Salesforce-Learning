## This is File Contains information of about Salesforce End-to-End Front Office 

Front office refers to the customer-facing side of a business. It includes sales, marketing, commerce, and customer service. 
It’s often discussed separately from back office functions like accounting, human resources, and supply chain, and so on.

Every Salesforce product is deeply integrated around a single source of data, shares metadata across channels, and is powered by an agentic layer called <b><i>Agentforce</i></b> to assist and connect employees, customers, and retail partners.
